Field Launcher

A working mockup of the home screen on a 960×540dp TV canvas, in the site's field-notebook language. Click the screen, then drive it with ← ↑ → ↓, Enter and Esc, or use the remote. Apps sit in a four-column grid that ends in an All apps tile; hold Enter on an app for its menu (Move, Pin to Home, Unpin from Home). The Tools row at the bottom holds the sleep timer, the viewing log and the readout; the margin note under the title is yours to edit. Turn on build owners to see which agent ships each part.
